## Documentation preferences

- Never add language-specific explanations or sections to general documentation. Keep them in language-specific pages or release notes. A supported-language table entry is sufficient when adding a language.
- Keep reference pages concise: syntax, values, defaults, and essential behavior. Do not add extended examples or walkthroughs; link to the relevant guide instead.
- Keep setup instructions and extended examples in the dedicated guide. Link to it from related pages rather than repeating the explanation.
